What Is Discipleship?

It's Not a Class.
It's a Life.

"Go therefore and make disciples of all nations… teaching them to observe all that I have commanded you." Matthew 28:19–20

Discipleship is the lifelong process of becoming more like Jesus — in how you think, how you love, how you serve, and how you invest in the people around you. It's not a membership card or a completed course. It's a daily decision to follow Jesus further.

At Christ Church, we believe this journey looks different for everyone — but it's never meant to happen alone. Wherever you are right now is the right place to start. PATHways ensures every person knows where they are, what to do next, and who walks with them.

The Discipleship Journey
P
Participate

You show up. You explore. You belong. Faith begins with presence — every journey starts with a first step through the door.

A
Apply

You commit. You go deeper. You join a community, build spiritual habits, and begin putting what you believe into how you actually live.

T
Take Action

You get off the sidelines. You discover how God wired you — and use those gifts to make a real difference for others.

H
Help Others

You give it away. You lead, mentor, invest in someone else's story — and the discipleship cycle begins again in someone new.

You are somewhere on this journey. Your next step is below.
