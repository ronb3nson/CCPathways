# PATHways brand

The shared reference for everything PATHways: the landing page, the brochure and any future pieces. When a value changes here, update `site/css/tokens.css` to match.

## The four areas

| Area | Tagline | Letter | Color |
|---|---|---|---|
| Participate | Belong | P | `#7FB3E5` |
| Apply | Become | A | `#14A8C2` |
| Take Action | Serve | T | `#F4AF00` |
| Help Others | Multiply | H | `#DE2A50` |

Help Others means making disciples who make disciples, not leadership. Treat the four as ways to grow, not ranked stages.

Letter circles use dark text (`#111111`) on Participate, Apply and Take Action, and white text on Help Others, for contrast.

## Type

- **Headings:** Gill Sans, semibold. Fallback: Lato.
- **Body:** Proxima Nova. Fallback: Montserrat.
- **Buttons:** Montserrat semibold, uppercase.
- **Serif:** Charis SIL, the serif of the PATHways report (free, SIL Open Font License). Italic for the emotional words in a heading ("your *walk with Jesus*", "your *next step*") and for short descriptions; regular for the definition of discipleship.
- **Report headings:** the report uses Montserrat ExtraBold; the site's sample report page does too.

Gill Sans and Proxima Nova are paid fonts. Confirm the license before embedding them in print files or self-hosting them.

## The definition

> Discipleship is **walking with Jesus** together — becoming like Him, living like Him, and helping others do the same.

Set it in Charis SIL with *walking with Jesus* in bold, and mark each area's phrase in that area's color: a light tint behind it and a 3px line under it. *together* is Participate, *becoming like Him* is Apply, *living like Him* is Take Action and *helping others do the same* is Help Others. This matches the opening of the PATHways report.

Each area in a few words, from the report: Belong, being known and connected in a church family. Become, growing in your own walk with Jesus. Serve, using what God has given you to meet needs. Multiply, making disciples who make disciples.

## Voice

- Each person's walk with Jesus is their own. Say "your walk with Jesus", "your next step" and "where you are today".
- Never say "the path", "choose your path" or "everyone is somewhere on the path". Never describe the four areas as levels or stages to climb.
- "Follow Jesus" is the main invitation. Use "walk" and "journey" for the imagery around it, and "journey" sparingly.

## Imagery

The theme is a day's walk: dawn at the start, night at the end. The pieces are a winding sandy footpath, hill lines, pines and oaks, cairns, wooden trail marker posts with blazes in the area colors, fingerpost signs, waypoint diamonds, lanterns and campfires. Keep it calm and illustrated, not busy, and show rather than tell: if a sentence can become a visual, make it one.
