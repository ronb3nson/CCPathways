What PATH stands for

Designed around four stages of growth.

P
Participate — Find connection, build relationships.
Explore faith, find community, and take your first steps at Christ Church.
A
Apply — Learn to follow Jesus, grow spiritually.
Commit to growing — Life Groups, daily disciplines, and faith put into practice.
T
Take Action — Use your gifts, make a difference.
Get off the sidelines — serve teams and community impact inside and beyond the church.
H
Help Others — Lead others, help others grow.
Reproduce what you've received — mentor, lead a group, and disciple newer believers.
